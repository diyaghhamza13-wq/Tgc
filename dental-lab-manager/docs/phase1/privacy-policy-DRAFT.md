**Dental Lab Manager — Privacy Policy**
Last updated: [DEPLOYMENT DATE]

Dental Lab Manager is a production and workflow management application for dental
laboratories, developed by Hamza Diyagh. It is used to follow manufacturing orders
(crowns, bridges, abutments, etc.) through the laboratory's production stages. It is not
a patient record system and it does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice or any
clinical function. This policy explains which data the app processes, why, and what
rights you have.

## 1. Who is responsible

Each laboratory that registers in the app decides which data it enters about its
employees and clients (dentists and clinics), and is responsible for that data (data
controller).

Hamza Diyagh provides and operates the application and processes this data only on behalf
of the laboratories, to provide the service (data processor). Contact:
diyaghhamza13@gmail.com.

## 2. Data we process

- **Laboratory:** name, code, contact details, address, city and logo.
- **Employees:** full name, employee code, department, role, specialty, and optionally
  phone, email and profile photo; last login time. PINs are never stored in readable
  form: only a one-way bcrypt hash is kept.
- **Dentists / clients:** name, clinic, phone, email, address, city and notes entered
  by the laboratory.
- **Cases (manufacturing orders):**
  - a case number generated by the app;
  - an optional order reference given by the clinic;
  - the dentist/client, dates, priority and status.
  - **Manufacturing specification:** which tooth positions the work concerns (FDI
    numbers), missing teeth and bridge layout, implant specifications (brand, system,
    connection, platform, dimensions, scan body, Ti-base, angulation), restoration type,
    material, shade and manufacturing notes.
  - Production history, quality-control results and delivery records.
- **Production files:** 3D files used to design and manufacture the work (STL, PLY,
  OBJ, 3MF and 3Shape DCM meshes).
  - Files are stored under a random identifier.
  - The file name shown in the app keeps only technical words (for example
    "scan_upper_prep_16.stl"). Other words, such as a person's name, are removed.
- **Technical data:**
  - security and audit logs of actions performed in the app (who did what and when);
  - the hosting provider's server logs (for example IP addresses), kept by the provider
    for security.

**What the app does not collect.** Since [DEPLOYMENT DATE], the app does not ask for or
accept:
- patient names or other patient identifiers, birth year, gender or patient notes;
- patient photos, CBCT/DICOM images, scanned prescriptions (images or PDF) or videos.

The app rejects these files.

## 3. Data recorded before [DEPLOYMENT DATE]

Earlier versions of the app asked for a patient name for each case and accepted
attached files of any type, including photos and videos. This earlier data has not been
deleted. It is handled as follows.

- **Earlier patient names**
  - They remain stored, but they are **no longer shown anywhere in the app.**
  - **No laboratory user can see, search, export or change them in the app, including
    the laboratory's administrators.**
  - They remain linked to the cases they were entered for, and they are deleted
    automatically if the laboratory deletes its account.
  - A laboratory that needs to see a copy of its earlier patient names, or wants them
    deleted, must ask us (see section 7).
- **Earlier attached files**
  - Photos, videos, CBCT/DICOM images, PDFs and other files that are not 3D production
    files can now be seen and downloaded **only by the laboratory's administrators.**
    The administrators can review them and delete them in the app.
  - Earlier 3D production files remain available to the employees who work on the case.
  - The original names of earlier files have been replaced by technical labels (for
    example "photo.jpg"). This applies to the app, the stored file locations, the
    production history and the audit logs. The content of the files has not been
    changed.

## 4. Why we use it

The data is used only to provide the service:
- managing manufacturing orders;
- coordinating production between employees;
- notifications and reports;
- keeping the laboratory secure.

We do not sell data, do not show advertising, do not use third-party analytics or
tracking, and do not use the data for any other purpose.

## 5. Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) in the
EU-West-2 (London) region. The web version is served by Netlify. These providers process
data only to host the service.

Each laboratory's data is isolated: employees of one laboratory can never access another
laboratory's data. Inside a laboratory, access depends on each employee's role and
permissions. Files are private and can only be opened by authorised employees through
temporary links. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

**Backups.** We keep encrypted backups of the database:
- in a separate Supabase project in the same region (London);
- the backup file cannot be read without a private key held only by the developer;
- backups are kept for at most 30 days, then deleted.

**Developer access.** As the operator, the developer has technical access to the
database. This access is used only to run, secure and repair the service, and to carry
out the requests described in section 7.

## 6. Health-related information

The app does not collect patient names, but some of the manufacturing information still
relates to the patient who will receive the work:
- the tooth positions, missing teeth and implant details;
- the 3D scans, which are a model of part of a patient's mouth.

The dentist who ordered the work can link the case to their patient. Under laws such as
the GDPR (Europe) or law 09-08 (Morocco), this information can therefore be considered
health-related personal data.

Laboratories must:
- **not** enter patient names or medical information in order references, notes or
  file names;
- have a lawful basis for processing this information under the laws that apply to them.

The app shows these reminders where text is entered, but it cannot check free text.

## 7. Requests about earlier patient names and other data

The app has **no button** for exporting or deleting earlier patient names. Requests are
handled **manually** by the developer:

1. **Who can ask:** the laboratory's administrator. Write to diyaghhamza13@gmail.com
   with the laboratory code and the request: a copy of the earlier patient names, or
   their deletion.
2. **Verification:** before doing anything, we confirm the request with the laboratory
   using the contact details registered for the laboratory in the app. We do not act on
   a request we cannot confirm.
3. **A copy:** we prepare an encrypted file with the laboratory's earlier patient names
   and the case numbers they belong to. We send it in a way agreed with the laboratory,
   and the password separately. We never send patient names in a plain e-mail.
4. **Deletion:** we delete the laboratory's earlier patient names from the database. The
   cases themselves (manufacturing data) remain. Copies in backups disappear when those
   backups expire (section 5).
5. **Timing:** we answer within 30 days.

**Patients** cannot be identified by us; we hold no patient contact details. A patient
should contact their dentist or the laboratory, which can then ask us as described
above.

## 8. How long data is kept

Data is kept as long as the laboratory uses the service.
- An employee can delete their own account at any time (Profile → Delete my account).
- A laboratory administrator can delete the whole laboratory and all its data,
  including files, accounts and any earlier patient names (Lab profile → Delete this
  laboratory).
- Deletion is permanent. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire, after
  at most 30 days.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data:
- **Employees:** through their laboratory administrator, or directly in the app.
- **Dentists and patients:** contact the laboratory that holds the data; the laboratory
  can ask us as described in section 7.

You can also write to diyaghhamza13@gmail.com.

## 10. Children

The app is intended for professionals and is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its update date.
